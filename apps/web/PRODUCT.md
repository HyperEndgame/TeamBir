# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

A mixed audience arriving at the corporate root site (teambir.com) rather than one of the six business subdomains: people evaluating Team BIR for a contract or purchase across materials, construction, transport, events, or travel; job seekers and subcontractors sizing up Team BIR as an employer or bid partner; and partners, press, or curious locals who want to understand the company as a whole before dealing with any one of its businesses. Whatever the entry point, the job to be done is the same — quickly trust that this is one credible, established Tennessee company, then find the right door in.

## Product Purpose

Team BIR consolidates six previously separate local Tennessee business sites — materials, luxury events/condominiums, transport, developments, and the travel plaza — under a single branded portal. The root site exists to route visitors into the right business and to make the whole family of companies read as one reliable, established operation rather than six disconnected small businesses. Success is measured in calls and quote requests flowing into the individual companies.

## Positioning

One Tennessee family business, built on reliability and reputation — the same name customers, partners, and subcontractors can trust whether the job is moving rock, building a home, hauling freight, hosting an event, or fueling up on the road.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: "Explore Our Companies" (routes into the business grid) is primary; "Contact Us" is the fallback for visitors not ready to pick a specific business yet.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: this isn't a single small operation — it's one family running six real, established Tennessee businesses.
- Belief ladder: (1) this is a real, multi-vertical company, not a single small outfit; (2) the specific business I need (materials, transport, construction, events, travel) is credible and properly licensed; (3) reaching out is a safe, easy next step.
- Proof on hand: none yet. Lean on the licenses and registrations already carried in `site-config.ts` (TN Contractor License #80985, USDOT 717687) and the founder story until testimonials, client logos, or press are available.

## Brand Personality

Bold and industrial — strong, hardworking, dependable, never flashy. The existing dark, cinematic, gold-accented visual language (Framer Nouva-inspired, per PLAN.md) is the confirmed direction; new work should extend it rather than soften it toward something more corporate-polished or startup-bright.

## Anti-references

Not a generic small-business template — no stock photography, no cookie-cutter WordPress-contractor layout. Also not a sterile enterprise site or a flashy SaaS/tech-startup look; the personality is working-Tennessee-company confident, not corporate-cold or gradient-blob trendy.

## Design Principles

One company, six trades: every page should reinforce that these are one reliable family business, not six disconnected websites, even as content and imagery differ per vertical.

Reliability over flash: bold industrial visuals carry the brand, but copy and CTAs stay grounded and specific — no SaaS-style hype language.

Fastest path to a call: every page's real job is getting the right visitor to the right business's contact point with minimal friction.

Credibility through specifics: licenses, addresses, phone numbers, and the founder's story stand in for testimonials until real proof assets exist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: sufficient contrast against the dark palette, full keyboard navigation, alt text on all imagery (especially the hero eagle art), and reduced-motion alternatives for scroll and parallax effects.
